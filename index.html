About

A work-in-progress human with a habit of asking the wrong questions

Still loading... Just like most people's self-awareness.

I write, build, unbuild, and leave things half-finished so they can breathe. There's something beautiful about incompleteness — it suggests possibility, growth, the audacity to believe that tomorrow's version of yourself might see what today's cannot.

This isn't about career achievements or polished success stories. It's about the messy, contradictory process of becoming — the uncomfortable questions that keep you awake, the projects that fail beautifully, and the slow realization that maybe the point isn't to arrive anywhere at all.

If you're looking for someone who has it all figured out, you're in the wrong place. If you're curious about minds that wander, question everything, and find profound meaning in the spaces between certainty and doubt — welcome to the disturbance.
